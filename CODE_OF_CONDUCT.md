# Code of Conduct

SortLab is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Examples of expected behavior:
- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome beginners and questions.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give feedback that helps others learn.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that violate this code. Maintainers may clarify or enforce these standards as needed.
